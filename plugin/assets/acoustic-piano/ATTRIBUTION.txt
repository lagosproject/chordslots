Acoustic Piano in ChordSlots uses recordings from Salamander Grand Piano V3,
by Alexander Holm, played on a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

Original: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
FLAC source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/master/Samples
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

ChordSlots selected the v8 recordings of A0, D#1, A1, D#2, A2, D#3,
A3, D#4, A4, D#5, A5 and D#6. The stereo recordings were mixed to mono,
resampled from 48 to 44.1 kHz, shortened to 3–5 seconds with a 650 ms
end fade, and encoded as 16-bit FLAC.
