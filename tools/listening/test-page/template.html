ChordSlots listening test

Welcome

You will hear short chord loops on a piano-like sound and answer a few quick questions. There are no right or wrong answers. Answer from your first impression. If you hear no difference, say so: that is a useful answer. It takes about 45 minutes. Use headphones at a comfortable volume.

Advanced: use rendered audio instead of the built-in sound

Optional. Pick a folder with .mp3/.wav files named like the stimuli (for example pair05_A.mp3). Leave empty to use the built-in sound.
